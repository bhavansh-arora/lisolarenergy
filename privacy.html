Privacy, in plain English.

The information this site asks for, why we ask, and what we do with it.

Information you give us

When you request a quote or send a message, we collect what you enter: your name, phone, email, property address, monthly bill estimate, goals and preferred appointment time.

Why we need it

To serve you, and nothing else: to prepare your estimate, schedule and carry out your site assessment, send your proposal, and keep you updated on your project. It is never sold, rented or traded.

Saved on your device

The quote form saves your answers in your browser so you can pick up where you left off, and keeps your tracking code so you can check your project from this device. You can clear this any time by clearing your browser’s site data, or by pressing “Start over” on the quote page.

Calls, texts and email

If you agree to be contacted, we may call, text or email you about your quote and project. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or ask us to stop at any time.
