How the math works

No black box. Just math.

01 · Usage

Your bill becomes kilowatt-hours.

Monthly bill ÷ your rate = the energy you use. That’s what your system needs to produce.

$ → kWh
02 · Size

Sun hours set the system size.

Yearly energy ÷ (sun hours × 365 × 80% efficiency) = the kilowatts of panels you need.

kWh → kW
03 · Savings

Rising rates, honestly modeled.

We project utility prices rising each year and panels losing 0.5% a year, then compare both paths.
